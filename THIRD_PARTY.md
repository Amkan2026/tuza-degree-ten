# Third-party code

The verification archive includes unchanged ancillary code and certificate data from Anish Gupta, *Tuza's conjecture for graphs of maximum degree at most seven*, [arXiv:2608.06538](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.06538). The [arXiv submission](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.06538) identifies the license as [Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/). The retained ancillary files keep their original authorship and bytes, including copies in the nested degree-eight archive. No endorsement by Gupta is implied.

The repository contains the computational supplement only. It does not include Gupta's article, Puleo's article, or the degree-ten manuscript. Gregory J. Puleo's *Tuza's Conjecture for Graphs of Maximum Average Degree Less Than 7*, [arXiv:1308.2211](https://arxiv.org/abs/1308.2211), supplies cited mathematical results but no redistributed article source.

[Archive provenance](verification/archive-provenance.json) lists the omitted manuscript and editorial files, the original and public archive hashes, and the nested archive changes. All retained computational sources, inputs, certificates and historical reports preserve their original bytes.
